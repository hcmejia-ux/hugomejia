Leadership Philosophy

Product development teams are best supported by servant leadership

  • Prioritizing team needs, growth, and wellbeing, rather than top-down control
  • Development plans driven by craft-oriented frameworks
  • Partnering to set clear and attainable performance objectives

Design is a discipline rooted in empathy

  • Align experience with customer needs, technology best practices and business goals
  • Accessibility, ethics, and inclusion as principles to guide and inform work for customers

Design must prioritize outcomes over outputs

  • Constantly pursuing measured and tangible progress
  • Fostering collaboration from all disciplines and stakeholders
  • Performing with disciplined strategy and tactics

Design is a natural partner to Product, Engineering and Executives

  • To support and de-risk decisions
  • To align tactics
  • To educate the organization about customers
